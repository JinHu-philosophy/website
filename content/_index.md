
# Tao Te Ching and Dao Theory
**《道德经》与“道”理论**



## Binary Dynamic Balance Theory
**《二元动态平衡理论》**

**Author: JINHU ZHOU**

This book establishes the Binary Dynamic Balance Theory, a systematic reinterpretation of the Tao Te Ching. Rooted strictly within the original text of Laozi, it first elaborates the material origin of the Dao and the underlying logic of human nature. Upon this foundation, it extracts three foundational laws that govern the evolution of all things: the inverted tree‑like hierarchical structure, the law of causal circulation, and the law of binary dynamic balance. These three principles couple and operate as an integrated whole, forming a unified analytical framework to decode the Dao.

Breaking away from traditional readings that reduce the Tao Te Ching to mysticism, self‑cultivation or tactical stratagem, this work reconstructs Laozi’s thought as an objective, self‑consistent system theory of cosmic and social evolution. It reveals how binary opposites emerge, interact, maintain dynamic equilibrium, and trigger structural transitions across all layers of existence, from the primordial generation of the universe to the operation of human society.

Volume One lays the foundational theory, Volume Two carries out full‑chapter interpretation of the original scripture, and Volume Three realizes summary and comprehensive practical application of the whole theoretical system.



> Volume One – Foundation Theory：Volume One excavates and sorts out the fundamental logic of the Dao derived from the original text of the Tao Te Ching. It sets forth the core theoretical framework, including the three foundational laws that form the cornerstone of the whole system, laying the theoretical bedrock for the subsequent volumes.

> Volume Two – Full‑chapter Interpretation：Volume Two carries out a comprehensive, chapter‑by‑chapter interpretation of the full original scripture of the Tao Te Ching. It applies the theoretical framework built in Volume One to parse each chapter of Laozi’s work.

> Volume Three – Balance Practice：Volume Three provides the summary, generalization and synthesis of the complete theoretical system. It demonstrates how to deploy the Binary Dynamic Balance Theory for comprehensive analysis and practical application across different fields.
