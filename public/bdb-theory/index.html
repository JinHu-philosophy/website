The ancient Chinese Yi tradition, grounded in the primordial insight of yinyang reciprocity and oppositional coexistence, caught sight of the rudimentary underlying laws governing the workings of all things in the cosmos. The Tao Te Ching, by contrast, strips away the external accretions of divinatory arts and metaphysical speculation attached to traditional Yi learning, stepping beyond empirical appearances and conventional interpretations. It distills a primordial, timeless governing principle that transcends any single era and permeates all phenomena. This ancient core of the Dao has undergone modern academic restructuring, systematization and logical articulation through Binary Dynamic Balance Theory (BDB Theory), yielding a general evolutionary mechanism applicable to all selforganizing systems in the universe.

The foundational mechanism of this theory is logically consistent and universally applicable across all domains: all entities subsist through the coexistence of dualities, flourish through their mutual generation, and stabilize through their reciprocal restraint. Within a system, the weighting of its dual components shifts continuously; when variables cross a critical threshold, a state transition is triggered. Every imbalanced, skewed or polarized systemic configuration carries an endogenous tendency to revert toward steady state. This underlying principle operates indiscriminately across four domains: the natural cosmos, living organisms, human cognition and reasoning, and human social communities. It constitutes an allencompassing foundational paradigm that supports crossdomain deduction.

Surveying the intellectual lineage of humanity, Karl Marx developed his system of dialectical thought independently, grounded in the productive structures and social realities of modern industrial civilization. We advance no claims of textual derivation, historical presumption or cultural transmission. Judging purely from logical architecture and objective evolutionary regularities, the primordial Dao thought of Taoism and Marxian dialectics share an identical underlying structure, converging upon the same objective laws of cosmic evolution.

Marx’s dialectical theory represents a concrete, branchlevel deduction of this universal foundational principle, situated within the specific historical context of modern socioeconomic relations and class production. Its core aligns closely with the internal logic of Binary Dynamic Balance Theory: iterative transformation of social formations cannot be forced into completion when preconditions remain insufficient and systemic thresholds are unmet. Social renewal consists fundamentally in dialectical sublation, targeting exploitative and dysfunctional old relations of production, rather than erasing humanity’s innate individual disposition and drive for selfrealization.

A core conclusion derivable from the higherorder framework of Binary Dynamic Balance Theory is this: even if human society reaches the ultimate stage of material abundance, the dual impulse toward individual particularity and selfaspiration will never vanish. It will merely evolve from the lowerorder contest over subsistence resources and material interests toward higherorder expressions of selfactualization, value realization and spiritual fulfillment. The public and the private, the collective and the individual, order and freedom, form the permanently coexistent core duality of human social systems. They generate and constrain one another; neither can be eliminated or swallowed entirely by its counterpart. Unidirectional polarization inevitably destabilizes and collapses the system.

Drawing upon this universal model of systemic evolution for strictly academic deduction: contemporary social institutions and governance arrangements worldwide are transient equilibrium configurations forged through the interplay and dynamic checks of multiple internal forces, corresponding to given historical stages, productive capacities and local civilizational traits. Prevailing institutional structures can adapt to developmental thresholds of their respective eras and sustain relatively stable dynamic cycles through contestation and negotiation. Other institutional forms, by contrast, suffer from imbalanced dual structures, rigid weighting mechanisms and misalignment with relevant thresholds. They cannot sustain longterm benign operation marked by selfstabilization, selfconsistency and selfrepair, and will inevitably undergo selftransformation as the system evolves.

All assessments and deductions set forth herein proceed exclusively from the logical framework of Binary Dynamic Balance Theory. They amount to pure academic reasoning and summary of objective regularities, free from subjective stances, institutional preferences or political bias. Entirely independent of ideology, geographic partisanship and temporal prejudice, this work presents neutral conclusions for interdisciplinary inquiry.

At the level of theoretical hierarchy, the primordial thought of the Tao Te Ching and Binary Dynamic Balance Theory rank as firstorder universal principles of cosmic and systemic foundations. They possess the capacity to subsume, explain, verify and deduce all branch theories tied to concrete domains. Intellectual frameworks born within particular historical contexts and confined to a single social sphere constitute secondorder, contextbound conclusions. They cannot in turn subsume or circumscribe this timeless, allpervading generative mechanism.

The ultimate mission of Binary Dynamic Balance Theory is the modern translation and systematic advancement of classical Chinese primordial Dao wisdom. It unpacks the ancient, intuitive, experiential conception of the balancing Dao into definable, quantifiable, verifiable, crossdomain and globally conversable modern intellectual tools. It establishes a complete hierarchical framework: Classical Primordial Wisdom — Modern Systemic Logic — Universal Contextual Application, supplying a neutral, foundational interpretive system for evolution across nature, cognition, society and civilization.

Complete Hierarchical Framework of the Theory: Micro Cognition — Meso Systems — Macro Civilization

Binary Dynamic Balance Theory establishes a coherent, integrated, selfconsistent explanatory system spanning three tiers. It covers the full evolutionary logic from individual reasoning and systemic operation to the transformation of human civilization, furnishing a complete architecture for ongoing theoretical refinement, practical deployment and crossdomain inference.

I. The MicroCognitive Tier

At the microcognitive level, Binary Dynamic Balance Theory functions as a complete cognitive framework and methodology of thought. Its core logic runs as follows: by unpacking the coexistent, oppositional dualities driving the evolution of all phenomena, one may discern the underlying generative mechanisms of systemic operation and event development. This permits precise identification of evolutionary trajectories and forecasting of developmental pathways. On this basis, one may deliberately harness the laws of systemic equilibrium, and even intervene by introducing new conditions or adjusting core variables to reshape the direction and final outcome of development. This yields a closed cognitive loop: discerning regularities and forecasting evolution, then intervening actively to redirect outcomes.

II. The MesoSystemic Tier

At the mesosystemic level, the natural cosmos, ecological life, human communities and all selforganizing systems are evolutionary systems anchored in the three primordial principles of Binary Dynamic Balance. The operational logic of all concrete systems is a tangible manifestation of primordial equilibrium principles within a specific field of conditions.

The dynamic operation of every system unfolds within the integrated framework of intrinsic dual structure plus contextual environmental variables. Within the progressive logic of causal cycles, all systems evolve following a treelike hierarchical structure. Continuous mutual generation and restraint between intrinsic dualities, alongside shifting weight distributions, repeatedly push the system past tiered thresholds shaped jointly by internal and external contextual variables. When a system crosses its existing threshold boundary, structural transition occurs, and a new steadystate equilibrium is reconstructed at the higher tier.

This unified operational schema governs the generation, circulation, transformation and reconfiguration of concrete systems ranging from physical matter and living ecosystems to social organizations and communal groups. It forms the shared dynamic evolutionary paradigm for all mesoscale systems.

III. The MacroCivilizational Tier

At the macrocivilizational level, human civilization unfolds through a clear sequence of hierarchical progression: from isolated individuals and households to clans, ethnic groups, peoples and polities, advancing upward along layered orders. The longterm evolutionary tendency of planetary civilization is the emergence of a unified global order akin to a broad framework of joint governance, culminating in civilizational unification.

Before reaching that configuration, however, humanity will remain for an extended period within the stage of polities, driven by collective particularity, where sustained contestation and evolution unfold. Deduced from this paradigm, communism as articulated by Marx cannot be fully realized within a single nationstate. Its realization presupposes humanity treated as one integrated planetary system. Only when material abundance is achieved on a global scale and the basis for resourcedriven conflict dissolves will this longterm vision acquire the necessary systemic preconditions.

This will inevitably be an extraordinarily protracted evolutionary process. Only upon entering this phase will humanity’s particularistic drives release themselves from lowlevel competition for survival resources and transition toward higher spiritual and axiological ends. The public and the private constitute an eternally coexistent duality within civilizational systems; their coexistence never terminates. Particularity can never be eradicated: it is the root from which the public sphere emerges, and the endogenous source fueling the forward progress of civilization.
